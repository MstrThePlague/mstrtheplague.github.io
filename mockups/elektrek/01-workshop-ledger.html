The Workshop

Coachbuilding, for a quieter kind of travel.

We are not a production line. Each van begins with a long conversation and a sheet of paper, and ends several months later with a set of keys and a numbered maker's plate fixed inside the door.

In between, everything is made here: cabinetry cut from native hardwoods, upholstery in British wool, and an electrical system designed around the base vehicle's own battery rather than fighting it.
