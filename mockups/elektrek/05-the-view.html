Bude · North Cornwall

Elektrek makes electric camper vans to private commission. Each is designed around a single owner and built by hand in our coastal workshop. We accept six commissions a year.

There is no range and no standard specification. We begin with the windows: where you will sit, what you will see, and how the light will fall inside. Everything else follows.
