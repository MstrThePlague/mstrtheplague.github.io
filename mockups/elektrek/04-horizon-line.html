Hello from the workshop

Made by hand, a few miles from the beach.

Elektrek began with a simple idea: the nicest way to see a coastline is slowly and silently. So we build campers on electric vans, and we build them properly.

There's no catalogue. We start with how you like to travel, then design everything around that, from where the kettle lives to which way the bed faces at sunrise.

— The Elektrek workshop, Bude
